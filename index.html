User guide

1. Upload two files

Upload your physical AV inventory spreadsheet on the left, and your ServiceNow CMDB export on the right. Both .xlsx, .xls and .csv are supported. Nothing leaves your browser — files are parsed and matched locally.

2. Check the column mapping

Once a file loads, this app guesses which column holds each field — serial number, asset tag, site, floor, room, hardware type, model, IP and MAC address. A small mapping row appears under the upload — fix any guess that looks wrong and the results update immediately. Only serial number is required; the rest are optional and simply won't be compared if a file doesn't have them.

3. Read the results

Matching tries serial number first, then falls back to asset tag, then MAC address, before giving up — so a device with a mistyped or blank serial can still be found. Every device lands in one category:

  • Matched — found in both (by serial, tag or MAC), and site/floor/room/model agree.
  • Mismatch — same device, but a field like floor, room or model disagrees.
  • Missing from CMDB — found onsite, no matching CI in ServiceNow.
  • Not found onsite — a CMDB record with no matching physical device.
  • Possible replacement — a CMDB record went missing, but a new device showed up in the same site/room/equipment slot.
  • Duplicate — the same serial appears more than once on one side.
  • Manual review — a physical record has no serial recorded at all.

4. Filter, override, and export

Click the small filter icon in the Site, Equipment or Model column headers to narrow the table to specific values. Use the Site and Hardware dropdowns on each row to mark a location as closed or a device as not in use — those rows are excluded from the health score and tile counts, and are dimmed in the table (with a "show excluded" link above it to bring them back). For anything missing from the CMDB, the suggested CMDB serial (taken from your physical inventory) shows in amber so you know what to enter in ServiceNow. If a device's serial isn't found at all, this app also tries matching by asset tag / CI name as a fallback. Use Export report to download everything as an .xlsx workbook.
